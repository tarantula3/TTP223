# TTP223
Stop using clunky physical buttons for your Arduino projects. Instead, why not use the TTP223 capacitive touch sensor. 

[![CAxqUMi57IQ](https://i.imgur.com/Souw7GQ.jpeg)](https://youtu.be/CAxqUMi57IQ)

Stop using clunky physical buttons for your Arduino projects. Instead, why not use the TTP223 capacitive touch sensor. The sensor detects your finger just like your phone screen. But the best part? It works through plastic, glass, and wood.
Capacitive touch is a technology that detects touch by sensing changes in electrical capacitance on a surface — like when your finger (which conducts electricity) touches a screen or button and alters its electric field. It's the tech behind most smartphone screens and touch buttons (including the TTP223 module).
